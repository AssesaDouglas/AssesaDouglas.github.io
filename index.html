Start with the problem

Is your business growing, but the way you manage it is holding you back?

Many organisations do not need another complicated app. They need their everyday work to become visible, traceable and easier to manage.

Too many spreadsheets and paper records

Customer details, stock, payments and reports sit in different files. Staff re-enter information and management struggles to know which record is correct.

Solution: one organised system and shared records.

You cannot see what is happening in real time

Owners and managers chase updates by phone. Jobs, deliveries, field activity and approvals are difficult to track across teams or locations.

Solution: dashboards, status tracking and alerts.

Costs, stock or payments do not reconcile

Small errors accumulate when purchases, sales, stock movements, expenses and payments are recorded separately or late.

Solution: linked transactions and audit trails.

Work depends on one person remembering everything

Approvals, follow-ups and customer requests get delayed when there is no clear owner, deadline or history of what has been done.

Solution: assigned workflows and reminders.

Customers keep asking for updates

Teams repeatedly answer questions about orders, documents, invoices, deliveries and service requests because information is hard to retrieve.

Solution: customer portals and automated notifications.

You have software, but it does not fit your process

Off-the-shelf tools may be too rigid, too expensive or poorly matched to local workflows, mobile use and Kenyan payment practices.

Solution: tailored software and integrations.

Common questions

Before we start.

Useful answers for organisations considering custom software development in Kenya.

How much does custom software development cost in Kenya?

The cost depends on the workflow, number of user roles, integrations, reporting requirements and delivery stages. A focused tool is usually simpler to scope than a full ERP. After understanding your requirements, I can prepare a defined scope and estimate rather than give you a misleading one-size-fits-all price.

Can you build a system for my specific business?

Yes. The starting point is to understand how your business operates, what information it handles and where delays, errors or lack of visibility occur. The system is then scoped around those needs.

Can the system integrate with M-Pesa, SMS or other software?

Where suitable APIs and provider access are available, integrations can be planned for M-Pesa, SMS gateways, accounting tools, websites and other business services. Integration feasibility is confirmed during discovery.

Will my staff be able to use it on their phones?

Web applications can be designed responsively for phones, tablets and desktop computers. Offline operation is possible for selected workflows, but it needs to be designed and tested explicitly.

Do I need to know exactly what software I want?

No. Explain the work you need to manage and what is going wrong today. We can map the process together and determine whether you need a custom application, an ERP module, an integration or a simpler improvement.
